Recommendation

Three buy boxes for the Park City, UT STR market, each a region paired with the product that wins there

They are ordered from weakest to strongest by Top 10% hit rate: Mountain Cabins (Summit Park, Pinebrook & Pine Meadow), Heber Valley Group Homes, and Old Town & Deer Valley Ski Access. All three come from a seven-region cluster analysis of the market's 145 listings. The deep dives (comp sets, revenue tiering, amenity evidence) are not built yet. The Mountain Cabin box is on regulatory hold: Summit County has proposed banning nightly rentals in Summit Park and Tollgate Canyon (see Section 5).

What the Top 10% Have in Common

A hot tub is table stakes. All 15 Top 10% listings have one, against 55% of the rest of the market, and it carries the largest size-controlled revenue uplift of any amenity present in 10 or more listings (+69%). Game rooms (73% vs. 22%) and pool tables (67% vs. 11%) are the next-widest gaps. "Size-controlled uplift" compares median revenue with and without the amenity inside each bedroom bucket, so a big house's amenities aren't credited for its size.

Location vs. Size — Median Revenue by Region and Bedroom Count

Each cell compares a region against the whole market at the same bedroom count. Green means a real location premium and red a discount; greyed cells rest on a single listing.

Old Town & Deer Valley is the only region with a location premium at every size, and it widens as homes get bigger (+44% at 3BR, +103% at 4BR). Elsewhere, revenue follows size. Heber runs 22–47% below market through 4BR and only reaches the market median at 5BR+. Its edge there is volume, not rate: it holds 15 of the market's 31 5BR+ listings. Pine Meadow is the reverse: its small cabins beat the market by 61%, while its 5BR+ houses trail it by 23%.
